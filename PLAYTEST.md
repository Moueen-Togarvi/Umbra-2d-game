# Umbra 0.3 human playtest

Automated routes and capture runs are not a human playtest. Complete the campaign on the intended Android device, including a fresh app launch from Continue. Keep audio audible for one pass and muted for the ending timing check.

Record device/model, Android version, refresh rate, headphones/speakers, and whether the ARM64 or universal APK was installed.

For each chapter record time to first successful completion, deaths, confusing puzzle, boring stretch, audio problem, frame drops and exact bug location. In low passages release crouch while under the ceiling; at patrol cover compare crouching still, moving quickly and standing. Verify that nearby vampires can be heard from the correct side. Kill the app after a checkpoint and check Continue; use chapter replay separately.

Check pause, rope up/down, one vibration per press, vibration off, and Reset settings. Reset must preserve unlocked chapters and Continue. At the ending, check credit readability, the 60-second music/credits timeline and the red eyes afterward. Listen to music loop boundaries through headphones; numeric seam checks cannot replace listening. The generated [loop listening page](build/review-results/loop-review.html) starts each exported loop three seconds before its join.

After an unassisted first pass, restart immediately after pressing a lever or pedal. The unfinished puzzle should return to its initial state. In the late upper/lower sections, check that the control's effect and the return route are readable, and that Continue after the next checkpoint keeps the completed machinery and enemy outcomes.
