## Approved AI running footfalls

`sfx/step_run_1.wav` through `sfx/step_run_6.wav` are individual foot impacts
cut from the user's approved **third** ElevenLabs generation. The original
is preserved at `assets/design/audio/elevenlabs_running_take_3.opus`.
These generated sounds are not CC0; ElevenLabs' terms and the generating
account's plan apply. They contain no added material, cloth or room layers.

Generation history ID: `898g8kdkBNm6g0mwofw0`.
Rebuild them with `python3 tool/audio/build_sounds.py --running-only`.
They play at their own pitch, levelled to -24 dB, on grass and in the caves.

## Music

Chosen by the user from previews. The menu, chase and ending pieces are
fixed; the player picks the music under play from the backgrounds, in the
main menu's Music panel. Each loop is about three minutes cut from the body
of the piece and crossfaded end into start; rebuild with
`python3 tool/audio/build_sounds.py --music <folder of the downloads>`.
